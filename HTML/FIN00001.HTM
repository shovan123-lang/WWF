THE BUSHWHACKERS ARE BACK
Toronto, Maple Leaf Gardens | Segment 1, backstage (1:00)

Sean Mooney in the backstage interview area. Luke and Butch march in
with their arms swinging, nearly knocking him over. Butch flexes his
right arm and points at the elbow.

SEAN MOONEY: Bushwhacker Butch, you've been out since Edmonton with an
injured elbow. How does it feel to be back?

BUSHWHACKER BUTCH: Feels good as new, Mooney! The doctor said rest it.
We don't know how to rest! We're from New Zealand!

BUSHWHACKER LUKE: And tonight it's the Orient Express and Mr. Fuji. We
been waitin' a long time for this, mate. Fuji's got his cane. We got
our heads!

They both slap their foreheads, then lick Mooney on the side of the
face and march off. Mooney wipes his cheek with his sleeve.

SEAN MOONEY: The Bushwhackers are back. Lucky me.
