Match observations: 

SUPERSTARS OF WRESTLING
Episode 2
Aired: July 20, 1991 (syndication)
Matches taped: BC Place, Vancouver, British Columbia, July 7, 1991

One week after WrestleFest, Superstars opened with the footage everybody
wanted to see again: Hulk Hogan's big leg drop on Sgt. Slaughter in St.
Louis, then Sid Justice and Harvey Wippleman ambushing the champion and
Hacksaw Jim Duggan before the Legion of Doom made the save.

The Rockers opened the wrestling against Elisha McGuire and Lindsay
Snyder, and for once it was Marty Jannetty who finished it, flying off
the top with a cross body on Snyder for the pin at 6:58. Shawn Michaels
was first out of the ring afterward. Make of that what you will.

Hulk Hogan had a message for Sid: if he wants the WWF Title, he can go
to President Jack Tunney and sign his name like a man. Sean Mooney then
ran down the rest of WrestleFest, from the Undertaker's Tombstone on the
Ultimate Warrior to Tito Santana's win over the Mountie.

Sid Justice needed just 2:07 to powerbomb Edward James. Wippleman did
the talking afterward and called out Tunney by name: Hogan, Sid and the
belt at SummerSlam. Sid had one line for the camera. "The belt already
knows who it belongs to."

Jake Roberts, sitting in the dark with Damien's bag at his feet, told
the injured Warrior to rest up and trust old Jake.

The Beverly Brothers beat Jerry Simmons and Lindsay Snyder, back for a
second beating in the same hour, when Blake hit the reverse neckbreaker
at 4:37. The Beverlys kept up the attack after the bell until Owen Hart
and Koko B. Ware ran them off, with the Genius hiding behind the
timekeeper.

Jimmy Hart called Dino Bravo yesterday's news, promised the Natural
Disasters will be back when Earthquake is healthy, and let the Nasty
Boys run down the Rockers. Ted DiBiase and I.R.S. followed with a
warning for Virgil and Roddy Piper: the Million Dollar Belt is coming
home at SummerSlam.

Mr. Perfect beat Shane Douglas with the Perfect-Plex at 6:11, and the
young man from Pittsburgh made him work for it. Afterward Bobby Heenan
laid down a challenge: at SummerSlam, Perfect will defend the
Intercontinental Title against anybody.

After a look ahead to SummerSlam and a preview of tonight's card at the
Spectrum, the main event delivered. Tito Santana and the Warlord went
just over twelve minutes. Harvey Wippleman, now in the Warlord's corner
as well as Sid's, got involved against Tito, but El Matador survived the
Full Nelson and pinned the Warlord with the flying forearm at 12:04.
With Heenan's challenge still hanging in the air, the announcers
couldn't help noticing who had just won.

The Undertaker and Paul Bearer closed the show by candlelight with a
message for the Warrior, and a reminder to Jake Roberts that the
Undertaker noticed how late he was in St. Louis.
