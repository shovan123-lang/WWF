HARVEY WIPPLEMAN WITH MEAN GENE
Vancouver, BC Place | Segment 5 (1:00)

Mean Gene Okerlund stands at the interview set. Harvey Wippleman bounces
into frame in his too-small suit, waving a finger in Gene's face.

MEAN GENE: Harvey Wippleman, your man Sid Justice just went through that
opponent like he wasn't even there.

HARVEY WIPPLEMAN: Like he wasn't there, Okerlund? Nobody is there when Sid
Justice is in the ring! You saw it. One powerbomb, lights out, good night!

HARVEY WIPPLEMAN: And I'll tell you what I don't understand. Tomorrow night
in St. Louis, they've got Sgt. Slaughter in the main event. Sgt. Slaughter!
Sid Justice is the most dominant man in the World Wrestling Federation, and
he's not even on the poster.

MEAN GENE: Some would say Sid has to earn his way into a title match.

HARVEY WIPPLEMAN: Earn it? He's earning it every single night, Gene. Hulk
Hogan knows it. Jack Tunney knows it. And sooner or later, everybody in
this building is going to know it.

Wippleman storms off as Gene shakes his head.
