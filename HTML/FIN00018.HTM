Event Center set. Maple Leaf Gardens marquee graphic, then a head shot
for each match. Runs in the Toronto market; other markets get their
own local report. Produced after Philadelphia 7/20.

MOONEY: Toronto, the WWF is at Maple Leaf Gardens TONIGHT.

In the main event, the WWF Champion Hulk Hogan goes one-on-one with
the Warlord, and Harvey Wippleman says Sid Justice will be in the
Warlord's corner.

beat the Nasty Boys to become the new WWF Tag Team Champions. Tonight

against Power and Glory.

Mr. Perfect puts the Intercontinental Title on the line against SATO.

Sgt. Slaughter faces Hacksaw Jim Duggan. Plus: the Undertaker, Jake
"The Snake" Roberts against Haku, Shawn Michaels against Owen Hart,
Montreal's own Dino Bravo, the Mountie against Shane Douglas, and the
return of Bushwhacker Butch.

Tickets are still available at the Gardens box office. Back to you,
