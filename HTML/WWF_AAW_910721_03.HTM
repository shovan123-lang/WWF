STUDIO - BOBBY HEENAN (2:30)
Heenan walks onto the set in a sport coat and wipes off the guest
chair with a handkerchief before he sits.

GENE: Bobby Heenan. Welcome... I think.

HEENAN: Okerlund, I've seen nicer sets in a car wash. And Jim, did
the farm send you into town for feed and you got lost?

JIM: Nice to see you too, Brain.

HEENAN: I'm here for one reason. At SummerSlam, Mr. Perfect defends
the Intercontinental Title against anybody. Anybody! They've all
tried. Davey Boy Smith. Tito Santana. Owen Hart, twice. Send them all
on the same night, Gene. Mr. Perfect will beat every one of them and
be home in time for supper.

GENE: Brain, tomorrow night on Prime Time, your men Haku and the
Barbarian face the Legion of Doom. Any thoughts?

HEENAN: (waves a hand) Haku and the Barbarian are grown men, Gene.
They know where the ring is.

JIM: Sounds to me like you'd rather talk about Mr. Perfect than your
own boys.

HEENAN: I'd rather talk about Mr. Perfect than breathe, you overgrown
scarecrow. That's what a manager does. He goes where the gold is.

GENE: I wonder how Haku and the Barbarian feel about that.

HEENAN: (stands up) They feel lucky. Okerlund, this chair is wet.

Heenan walks off the set.

JIM: Gene, I don't think he likes us.
GENE: He doesn't like anybody, Jim. Except Mr. Perfect.
