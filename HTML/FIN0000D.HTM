THE NASTY BOYS WANT THEIR BELTS BACK
Toronto, Maple Leaf Gardens | Segment 5, backstage (2:00)

Sean Mooney stands in the backstage interview area. Jimmy Hart storms in
first, megaphone in hand, with Brian Knobbs and Jerry Sags behind him.
Knobbs has tape around his ribs. Sags is carrying an empty belt bag.

SEAN MOONEY: Jimmy Hart, Nasty Boys, last night in Philadelphia, after
almost an hour, the Legion of Doom became the new Tag Team Champions.
Tonight they make their first defense, against Power and Glory. Your
reaction?

JIMMY HART: My reaction? My reaction, Mooney, is robbery! Highway robbery!
Two falls! Two falls they took off my boys because a referee didn't like
the way I was cheering! I'm a manager! I'm supposed to cheer! And for
that, the Legion of Doom walks out of Philadelphia with OUR gold!

Hart blows into the megaphone. Mooney winces.

JERRY SAGS: Look at this bag, Mooney. Empty. You know how long that bag's
been full? You know what it's like to drive to Toronto with nothing in
it? I'll tell you what it's like. It's nasty.

BRIAN KNOBBS: Hawk. I hit you with everything I had. Three powerslams.
Fifty-six minutes. And you got lucky, because the man in the striped shirt
gave you two falls for free. That ain't seven. That's five. So you and
your buddy Animal can parade around Maple Leaf Gardens tonight with our
belts, but you better enjoy it, because you're only borrowing them.

BRIAN KNOBBS: We want our rematch. No Death Match. No referees with eyes
in the back of their heads. Just you two and us two. And when we get you,
we're gonna take the belts back, and then we're gonna take your spikes and
stuff them where the sun don't shine.

JERRY SAGS: Nasty!

JIMMY HART: You heard 'em! Jack Tunney, are you listening? The Nasty Boys
want a rematch!

Hart leads them off, still yelling into the megaphone. Sags turns back
and waves the empty belt bag at the camera.
