WWF ON THE ROAD
WWF Studio, Stamford, CT | Segment 13 (1:30)

Sean Mooney at the desk. A "WWF On The Road" graphic with a map of western
Canada.

SEAN MOONEY: The World Wrestling Federation just wrapped up a tour of
western Canada, and there's news from the road that will have an impact on
tomorrow night.

A still photo of Road Warrior Hawk leaping over the top rope at Sgt.
Slaughter.

SEAN MOONEY: In Regina, Saskatchewan, the Ultimate Warrior faced Sgt.
Slaughter in the main event. General Adnan got involved, but then Road
Warrior Hawk stormed the ring and attacked the Sergeant, and the referee
awarded the match to Slaughter by disqualification.

A photo of Big Boss Man hoisting the Mountie for the Boss Man slam.

SEAN MOONEY: The next night in Winnipeg, Hulk Hogan and Big Boss Man teamed
up against Sgt. Slaughter and the Mountie. Jimmy Hart tried to interfere,
but Big Boss Man pinned the Mountie with the Boss Man slam, a little
payback for the Mountie handcuffing him to the ropes in Calgary.

A photo of Virgil trapped in the Penalty.

SEAN MOONEY: Also in Winnipeg, Irwin R. Schyster made Virgil pass out in
the Penalty with help from Jimmy Hart. Virgil faces Ted DiBiase tomorrow
night at WrestleFest.
