MAIN EVENT - AAW EXCLUSIVE (taped Richfield 7/18)
SATO vs. Paul Roma
Finish: Roma goes up top. SATO follows him up and brings him down
with a superplex. SATO pins at 7:03. ***1/4
(9:30 with entrances and the replay)

Layout:
- Roma flexes on the turnbuckles. Hercules isn't at ringside. SATO
  bows to all four sides.
- Roma uses the power early: shoulder blocks and a press slam,
  posing after each one.
- SATO comes back with kicks, armdrags and a layback suplex for two.
- Roma cuts him off with a dropkick and goes to work: backbreaker,
  bearhug in the middle, a powerslam for two.
- Roma climbs to finish it. SATO is up first, climbs after him and
  brings him down with the superplex. One, two, three.
- Replay of the superplex from the hard camera.

Studio over the footage:
- GENE: SATO beat Shane Douglas twice in Canada this month, Jim, and
  both of them were barn-burners.
- JIM: That boy don't quit, Gene.
- GENE: And Paul Roma has something to prove after the Legion of Doom
  beat Power & Glory at WrestleFest.
- On the superplex, JIM: "From way up there! Gene, that's three for
  SATO!"
