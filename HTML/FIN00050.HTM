Location: Backstage of Madison Square Garden. 
Characters: Sean Mooney, Paul Bearer, The Undertaker
Context: Prematch comments from The Undertaker about tonights main event! 


[Sean Mooney stands in front of a black curtain, microphone in hand, 
looking uneasy.]

Sean Mooney:
“Ladies and gentlemen, I’m standing by with one of the most chilling 
figures in the World Wrestling Federation—The Undertaker—and his 
ever-creepy manager, Paul Bearer. Gentlemen, tonight, you face 
The Ultimate Warrior in a casket match. Paul Bearer, what kind of 
message do you have for Warrior?”

[Paul Bearer steps forward, clutching the urn, voice trembling with glee.]

Paul Bearer:
“Ohhhhhh yesssss, Sean Mooney! The time is drawing near! The Ultimate 
Warrior’s fate has been sealed! My Undertaker has crafted a casket just 
for him… lined with the cold velvet of the afterlife! Warrior may run, 
he may scream, but he cannot escape the darkness!”

[Undertaker slowly raises his head, eyes rolled back, speaking in a deep,
deliberate tone.]

The Undertaker:
“Warrior… your energy will fade. Your spirit will break. And when the 
final bell tolls… you will rest… in peace.”

[Paul Bearer lets out a ghostly moan, holding the urn high as fog rolls in.]

Sean Mooney (visibly shaken):
“Well folks, there you have it. The Undertaker is ready, and the 
casket awaits. Back to ringside…”
