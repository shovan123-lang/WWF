MR. PERFECT AND BOBBY HEENAN
Winnipeg, Winnipeg Arena (recorded 7/12) | Segment 8 (1:00)

Bobby Heenan and Mr. Perfect stand with Mean Gene. Perfect has the
Intercontinental title over his shoulder and is snapping a towel. Heenan is
already laughing.

BOBBY HEENAN: Owen Hart? Owen Hart! Gene, that's the best they could come
up with? A little brother looking for a handout. He couldn't beat Mr.
Perfect in Edmonton and he won't beat him in St. Louis.

MEAN GENE: Bobby, let's talk about tonight here in Winnipeg. Mr. Perfect
walked off the apron and left the Barbarian alone in the ring. Your own
man.

MR. PERFECT: Gene, let me explain something to you. I don't carry anybody.
Not the Barbarian. Not Owen Hart. Not anybody. I had a title defense
tomorrow, and I wasn't about to get hurt for a guy who couldn't make a tag.

BOBBY HEENAN: And the Barbarian understands that. It's business, Gene.
That's all. We're one big happy family.

MR. PERFECT: Owen Hart, tomorrow night I'm perfect again. Absolutely
perfect.

Perfect throws the towel at the camera.
