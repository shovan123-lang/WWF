HEENAN, PERFECT AND HAKU
Toronto, Maple Leaf Gardens | Segment 4, backstage (1:30)

A locker room door marked "PRIVATE." Bobby Heenan stands in front of it
with Mr. Perfect, who is chewing gum with the Intercontinental Title
over his shoulder. Sean Mooney approaches.

SEAN MOONEY: Bobby Heenan, Mr. Perfect, the Intercontinental Title is
on the line tonight against SATO.

BOBBY HEENAN: SATO. Last night he beat Hercules. Big deal. Hercules
couldn't beat a rug. Tonight SATO gets the greatest athlete in the
world, and he goes home on a stretcher with a souvenir program.

MR. PERFECT: SATO's a good wrestler, Mooney. Good. I'm Perfect. There's
a difference, and he's about to learn it.

Haku walks into the shot, taped up and ready to go.

HAKU: Heenan. Jake Roberts. Now. You come.

BOBBY HEENAN: Haku, my friend, my brother. I'm with the champion
tonight. You don't need me against a man who sleeps with a snake. Go
out there, do what you do, and I'll watch it on the monitor. I
promise.

Haku looks at Heenan, then at Perfect, then at the belt. He walks off
without a word.

BOBBY HEENAN: (to Mooney) He's fine. He loves me. Get out of here.
