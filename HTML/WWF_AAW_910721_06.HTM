WWF UPDATE - SEAN MOONEY (2:00)
Event Center set. A head shot over each item.

MOONEY: Here's a look at who's on the shelf, and who's on the way
back.

- The Ultimate Warrior injured his neck against the Undertaker at
  WrestleFest in St. Louis. Doctors have not said when he'll return.
- Ricky "The Dragon" Steamboat tore a ligament in his knee in Des
  Moines on July 5th and is expected to miss the rest of the year.
- Earthquake hurt his neck in Edmonton on July 9th and will be out
  until after SummerSlam.
- Greg "The Hammer" Valentine was also hurt in Edmonton, a neck
  injury. He hopes to be back in the second half of August.
- Some good news: Bushwhacker Butch, out with an injured elbow since
  Edmonton, has been cleared and returns this weekend.
- And the British Bulldog, Davey Boy Smith, is touring Japan and
  returns at the end of the month.

Gene, back to you.
