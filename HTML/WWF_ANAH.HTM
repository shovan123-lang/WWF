Match observations: 

Anaheim, CA
Anaheim Convention Center
July 6th, 1991 Average Ticket Price: $20.00
Attendance: 5,279 Gate $105,580

Anaheim fans showed up in force tonight to check out live WWF Action.

Our Main Event saw The Ultimate Warrior defeat The Undertaker in a 
Bodybag match! With Paul Bearer getting involved multiple times, The Warrior
clubbed him from the ring apron and eliminated him from the match.
Warrior would go on to roll the Undertaker into the bag and send the crowd 
home happy. 

In other action, Crush picked up a win for Demolition defeating Brutus
Beefcake in singles competition. Beefcake teased cutting the long locks
of Crush in the ring, but the strength of Crush ended that possibility. 

Jake Roberts and the Earthqake continued their rivalry tonight. Jake scared
the Earthquake when he slid Damien into the ring and the Hart family
took cover. Jake eventually hit his DDT finisher to seal the victory. 

In tag team action, the Islanders looked impressive making short work of
the Bushwackers. That match was taped for Coliseum Home Video.
