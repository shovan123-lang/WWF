INJURIES PILE UP IN EDMONTON AS WRESTLEFEST LOSES EARTHQUAKE

The night after Calgary drew $260,000, the WWF went up the road to the
Northlands Coliseum in Edmonton on Tuesday, July 9. The card rated *1/2, a
step down from Calgary's **, and the story wasn't the matches. Three
wrestlers got hurt, and two of them are booked on the WrestleFest pay-per-
view Sunday in St. Louis.

The worst news is Earthquake. He hurt his neck taking the Ultimate
Warrior's splash in the main event and is expected to miss about five
cards. With four house shows between now and Sunday, that should take him
off WrestleFest, where he was booked against Jake Roberts. Greg Valentine
hurt his neck in the tag match and is out about three cards. That leaves
Saskatoon, Regina and Winnipeg, and he should be back by Milwaukee on
Saturday. But Valentine is the challenger for Mr. Perfect's
Intercontinental Title on Sunday, after Davey Boy Smith was taken off the
show, so the office now has a neck injury on top of a replacement
challenger. Bushwhacker Butch hurt his elbow and is only expected to miss
one card.

Tenryu and Kitao beat Power & Glory at 14:18 for ***1/4, the match of the
night along with the Rockers. Tenryu pinned Paul Roma with a powerbomb.
That was the second strong showing for the SWS wrestlers in two nights,
after SATO's *** opener in Calgary, and Tenryu and Kitao go into their
WrestleFest match with Demolition looking like a real team.

The Rockers beat the Beverly Brothers at 16:57 (***1/4) when Michaels
pinned Beau with a side suplex. The Coach got involved against Jannetty.
The Rockers have worked hard on every show of this loop, and they get the
Nasty Boys for the tag titles on Sunday.

Mr. Perfect beat Owen Hart in a non-title match at 18:28 for **3/4. Perfect
won with a swinging neckbreaker after Bobby Heenan interfered. That's three
nights in a row Perfect has needed Heenan or gone long, and the rating was
well under what these two should do. Owen still came out looking good after
his Calgary match with Martel.

The surprise of the card was the Bushwhackers. They beat the Orient Express
at 17:03 for **3/4, which is the best number they've had in a while, even
if 17 minutes is a lot of Bushwhackers. Luke pinned Kato with the battering
ram. Butch's elbow injury came out of this one.

Virgil and Valentine beat Money Inc. at 14:11 (**3/4) when Virgil rolled up
IRS. Virgil has now cut IRS off in Calgary and pinned him in Edmonton, and
he gets Ted DiBiase one on one at WrestleFest. Koko B. Ware opened by
beating Skinner with the Ghostbuster at 10:11 (**1/4). Skinner hasn't been
featured since the Steamboat injury in Des Moines.

The bottom of the card was Sid Justice over Brutus Beefcake at 7:26, rated
-***1/4. Sid won with the powerbomb. That's the same rating Beefcake had
against Crush in Anaheim, so the common thread is obvious. Sid is about to
be pushed as Hulk Hogan's next opponent and needs better than this.

The main event was the Ultimate Warrior and Jake Roberts over the
Undertaker and Earthquake at 15:45, rated 1/4*. Warrior pinned Earthquake
with the splash, which is where Earthquake got hurt. After the bell, the
Undertaker and Paul Bearer jumped Warrior. Jake stood on the apron and
watched for a few seconds before he came in to help. Fans may not have
noticed, but it's worth watching. Warrior and Undertaker have now had four
matches this month, and none has rated better than *.

Bottom line: a step down from Calgary, with good work from Tenryu and
Kitao, the Rockers and the Bushwhackers and a bad main event and Sid match.
Three injuries in one night, two of them affecting WrestleFest. The office
has five days to fix the card.
