STEAMBOAT UPDATE / TYPHOON AND JIMMY HART / JAKE ROBERTS
WWF Studio, Stamford, CT and Winnipeg, Winnipeg Arena | Segment 15 (2:45)

Sean Mooney at the desk. Footage from Des Moines plays of Ricky Steamboat
crumpling to the mat holding his right knee.

SEAN MOONEY: An update now on Ricky "The Dragon" Steamboat. On July 5th in
Des Moines, Iowa, Steamboat tore the ACL in his right knee in a match
against Skinner. Steamboat is out of action indefinitely, and he'll need
surgery. The Dragon has asked to address the WWF fans in person, and he'll
do that tomorrow night at WrestleFest.

The scene cuts to Jimmy Hart with Typhoon, who towers over him. Hart is
screaming into his megaphone.

JIMMY HART: Jake Roberts! All month long you've been after Earthquake, and
now Earthquake is hurt! Well, baby, the Natural Disasters stick together.
Typhoon is coming to St. Louis to make you pay for every single thing you
did to his partner!

TYPHOON: Roberts, I'm not afraid of you, and I'm not afraid of your snake.
Bring the snake.

The lights go down. Jake Roberts sits on a stool in a dark corner, the
Damien bag at his feet. He speaks barely above a whisper.

JAKE ROBERTS: Typhoon. Earthquake. It doesn't matter which one of you shows
up. Same size, same bad attitude, same result.

Jake rests his hand on the bag. It moves.

JAKE ROBERTS: Damien is coming to St. Louis. Trust me.
