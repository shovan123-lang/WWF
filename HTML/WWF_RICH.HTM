Match observations: 

Richfield Coliseum
Richfield, Ohio
July 18, 1991
Attendance: 8,500
Average ticket: $12
Gate: $102,000

The WWF cameras rolled into the Richfield Coliseum for an All American
Wrestling and Prime Time Wrestling taping. Matches will air on the USA 
Cable network in the upcoming weeks. With Hulk Hogan and the
Ultimate Warrior off the card, the tag team division carried the night.

Brutus Beefcake needed just over three minutes to put Jerry Simmons to
sleep, but the post match haircut took twice as long. Virgil, still 
carrying the Million Dollar Belt he took off Ted DiBiase in St. Louis, 
made Dong Phillips submit to a cobra clutch in 2:06.
Virgil has a new hold, and DiBiase will want to see the tape. In the meantime,
I.R.S. got a ringside view when he ran down to attack Virgil after the match,
and grab the Million Dollar Belt. He took the belt and headed up the ramp,
only to be met by Rowdy Roddy Piper, who knocked him on his butt and gave the
title back to Virgil, as I.R.S. Retreated to the back.

SATO and Paul Roma had the crowd on its feet for seven minutes before
SATO brought Roma down from the top rope with a superplex for the win.
Three straight strong showings for the man from Japan.

On the Prime Time side, Tito Santana picked up where WrestleFest left off,
catching Dino Bravo with the flying forearm at 10:45. Jimmy Hart was livid
with Bravo after another loss, and effectively fired him on the spot! Dino
was having none of it, and pressed slammed Jimmy out of the ring. From here
on out, the big man from Canada walks alone! 

Jake Roberts dropped Hercules on his head with the DDT, and Hercules was still 
on the mat when Jake bagged up Damien and walked out.

The Rockers and Demolition went sixteen minutes of all-out war. Shawn
Michaels and Marty Jannetty, four days removed from the shove at Busch
Stadium, worked like a team again, and Ax and Smash answered every high-
flying move with raw power. The fight spilled to the floor, neither team
would let go, and referee Earl Hebner counted all four men out at 16:06.
Nobody won, and nobody left happy, especially Shawn Michaels who once again
was furious with Jannetty for not winning the match. Michaels left Jannetty
alone in the ring and walked to the back.

The Legion of Doom closed the televised portion by running through Haku and
the Barbarian. Hawk and Animal executed the Doomsday Device on Haku at
12:03, and Bobby Heenan was left screaming at both of his men on the way to
the back. From the looks of things on TV, Barbarian was slow to break up the
pin when Haku was out cold on the mat. 

After the cameras went off, Big Boss Man and Hacksaw Jim Duggan sent the
fans home happy over the Mountie and Typhoon. Jimmy Hart got involved
against Duggan, but Boss Man caught the Mountie with the Boss Man Slam for
the pin. The Mountie owes the Boss Man a night in jail, and that score
isn't settled yet.
