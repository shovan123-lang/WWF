THE LEGION OF DOOM ARE YOUR NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS

The Legion of Doom have been after the Nasty Boys all month, and on
Saturday night in Philadelphia they took the decision out of everyone's
hands. Hawk and Animal demanded a Death Match for the WWF Tag Team
Championship: no time limit, first team to seven falls. The Nastys and
Jimmy Hart agreed, and for almost an hour the Spectrum got a war.

The Legion went up early when Jimmy Hart couldn't keep his megaphone to
himself, and the referee gave the first fall to Hawk at 12:05. After that
it was Brian Knobbs who carried the Nastys on his back. He took Hawk down
with a forearm at 14:49, and three times he planted Hawk with the running
powerslam, the last one at 49:18, to keep the Nastys alive. Hawk kept
coming. Flying clotheslines at 18:13 and 29:01, a flying shoulderblock at
38:05, then a gorilla press at 54:06 that put the Legion at five. Seconds
later Hart went after Hawk again, and again the referee saw it. That was
six. At 55:59 Hawk came off the ropes with one more flying shoulderblock
and pinned Knobbs for the seventh.

Seven falls to four. After 55 minutes and 59 seconds, Hawk and Animal held
the belts over their heads in the middle of the Spectrum ring, and Jimmy
Hart was dragging both Nasty Boys up the aisle by their shirts.

HOGAN AND DUGGAN WIN, BUT SID NEVER TAGS IN

Hulk Hogan and Hacksaw Jim Duggan beat Sgt. Slaughter and Sid Justice when
Hogan dropped the leg on Slaughter at 15:23. The story was the man who
never got in. Every time Slaughter reached for a tag, Sid looked at Hogan
and stayed where he was. When Slaughter finally stretched for him, Sid
stepped off the apron and walked up the aisle with Harvey Wippleman. Hogan
followed him to the ropes and pointed. Sid stopped halfway and stared
back. Nobody touched anybody. Nobody had to.

SNUKA SPLASHES THE MOUNTIE

The Mountie had Jimmy Hart in his corner and the Carotid Control waiting,
and he still couldn't put Jimmy Snuka away. Hart got involved, the crowd
got louder, and at 12:44 Snuka came off the top with the Superfly Splash
and pinned him. The Mountie went for the shock stick after the bell. The
Big Boss Man came through the curtain before he could use it, and the
Mountie ran.

TITO CATCHES MARTEL

Rick Martel has been winning with his feet on the ropes all week. On
Saturday he tried it on Tito Santana, and the referee saw it this time
and kicked his feet off. Tito hit the flying forearm and pinned him at
10:37, on the same morning his win over the Warlord aired on Superstars.

THE ROCKERS BEAT DEMOLITION

Two nights after Richfield, the Rockers got their rematch with Demolition
and won it. Marty Jannetty took the beating for most of the match, Shawn
Michaels got the hot tag, and Michaels superkicked Smash for the pin at
15:14. Then he climbed the turnbuckle and celebrated alone. Jannetty was
still getting up.

VIRGIL AND PIPER TOP MONEY INC.

IRS reached for his briefcase, and Roddy Piper took it away from him.
Virgil rolled IRS up at 13:29, and Virgil and Piper had the win. Ted
DiBiase jumped Virgil after the bell, and Piper ran him off with his own
partner's briefcase. Virgil left with the Million Dollar Belt still
around his waist.

PERFECT KEEPS THE GOLD

Dino Bravo had Mr. Perfect in trouble until Bobby Heenan tripped him off
the ropes. Perfect hit the Perfect-Plex at 12:09 and kept the
Intercontinental Championship.

JAKE AND THE URN

Jake Roberts beat the Berzerker with the DDT at 8:50. As Jake left the
ring, Paul Bearer stood on the ramp holding the urn. Jake stopped, looked
at him, and gave a small nod. Then he walked out the other way.

THE GENIUS STRIKES AGAIN

High Energy saved the jobbers from the Beverly Brothers on Superstars that
morning, and the Beverlys made them pay for it. The Genius cracked Koko B.
Ware with his scroll behind the referee's back, and Blake finished Koko
with a hotshot at 12:54.

SATO OPENS IT

SATO beat Hercules with a belly-to-back superplex at 8:17 to open the
show.
