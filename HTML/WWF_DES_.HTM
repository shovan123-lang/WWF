Match observations: 

Des, Moines, IA
Veterans Memorial Coliseum
July 5th, 1991 Average Ticket Price: $20.00
Attendance: 4,500 Gate $90,000

Solid energy from the crowd in Des Moines tonight-no cameras but the stories
kept rolling. The Ultimate Warrior gets a victory over the Undertaker by 
DQ when Paul Bearer hit the Warrior over the head with the urn. 
The Undertaker choked him out until officials were able to pull him off.

We had a situation with Ricky Steamboat tonight. He had a freak injury
during his match with Skinner. Early indications were a torn ACL, which may
keep him out of action for an extended period of time. 

Mr. Perfect defended the IC title over Davey Boy Smith tonight. The mat
technician put on a show, although Iowa fans booed the champion out of the
building! Bobby Heenan mocked Davey Boy before he and Perfect went to the 
dressing room.
