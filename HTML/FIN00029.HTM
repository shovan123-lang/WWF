MEAN GENE WITH HULK HOGAN
Vancouver, BC Place | Segment 19 (2:00)

Hulk Hogan, out of breath and fired up, stands with Mean Gene. The crowd is
still buzzing from the attack after his match.

MEAN GENE: Hulk Hogan, Sgt. Slaughter and General Adnan ambushed you just
now, and if it weren't for Sid Justice, I don't know what would have
happened.

HULK HOGAN: Mean Gene, I gotta give it up for Sid Justice, brother. Sid
came down there and he had my back, and the Hulkster doesn't forget that.

HULK HOGAN: But Sgt. Slaughter and General Adnan just showed me what's
coming tomorrow night. They'll do anything to take this belt out of
America. So I'm gonna make sure nobody gets in the middle of this. And I
got just the man for the job.

Hogan looks off camera and points.

HULK HOGAN: The special enforcer for WrestleFest is a real American,
brother. Hacksaw Jim Duggan!

Jim Duggan charges into the frame, 2x4 in hand and thumb in the air.

JIM DUGGAN: HOOOOO! Slaughter! Adnan! You want to mess with the Hulkster,
you gotta come through me and this 2x4! USA! USA!

HULK HOGAN: Tomorrow night at Busch Stadium, whatcha gonna do, Sarge, when
Hulkamania and Hacksaw run wild on you?

Hogan and Duggan pose for the crowd. Off to the side, unnoticed, Sid
Justice watches.
