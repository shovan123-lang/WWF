MAIN EVENT (taped Vancouver 7/7)
Tito Santana vs. The Warlord
Finish: Tito escapes the Full Nelson, comes off the ropes with the
flying forearm, and pins Warlord clean. 
Layout:
- Warlord overpowers Tito early. Shoulder blocks and a bearhug,
  and he poses for the camera in the middle of it.
- Tito fires back with the punches in the corner, the crowd counting
  along, and a dropkick. Warlord cuts him off with a powerslam for a
  two count.
- Warlord locks in the Full Nelson. Tito's arm drops twice. On the
  third, Tito gets his feet on the ropes and pushes off. Both men go
  down.
- Tito catches Warlord coming in, Irish whip, flying forearm.
  One-two-three.

Commentary notes:
- Vince: Tito had the match of the night at WrestleFest, beating the
  Mountie. Now he's up against nearly 320 pounds of muscle.
- Savage: "The Warlord just put Jimmy Snuka to sleep in St. Louis
  with that Full Nelson, Vince. Nobody gets out of that!"
- After the pin, Vince: "Tito Santana got out of it! Bobby Heenan,
  you said ANYBODY. I think you may have just found your man."
- Savage: "Oooh yeah! El Matador has his eyes on the gold, Vince!"
- Tito celebrates with the fans as we go to the close.
