POST-MATCH - SATO (taped insert, Richfield 7/18, 1:30)

Interview area. Gene. SATO still breathing hard, a towel around his neck.

GENE: SATO, an impressive win over Paul Roma. And this Sunday at Maple Leaf Gardens, you get Mr. Perfect with the Intercontinental Title on the line.

SATO: Mr. Perfect is a great champion. I respect him. But every time he wins, Bobby Heenan helps him. In Toronto, I watch Heenan, and I beat Mr. Perfect. The belt comes home with me.

SATO bows to Gene and walks off.

GENE: Short and to the point. Mr. Perfect, you've been told.
