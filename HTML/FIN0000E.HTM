WRESTLEFEST REPORT - SEAN MOONEY (3:00)
Event Center set. Footage over each line.

MOONEY: The biggest crowd of the summer, 51,357 strong in St. Louis.
Here's what you missed.

- The Undertaker beat the Ultimate Warrior with the Tombstone.
  Warrior was helped from the ring with an injured neck, and Jake
  "The Snake" Roberts finally arrived, long after the bell. Doctors
  say Warrior will be out indefinitely.

- The Nasty Boys kept the WWF Tag Team Titles when Brian Knobbs and
  Jerry Sags got past the Rockers. Sags pinned Marty Jannetty with
  the flying elbow. Afterward, tempers flared between the Rockers.

- Mr. Perfect is still Intercontinental Champion. Owen Hart had him
  beaten, but Bobby Heenan got involved and Perfect stole it with a
  roll-up.

- Tito Santana beat the Mountie with the flying forearm. Many
  called it the best match of the night.

- "Million Dollar Man" Ted DiBiase beat Virgil, but Virgil left with
  the Million Dollar Belt, and Rowdy Roddy Piper made sure he kept
  it.

- Also: Demolition beat Tenryu & Kitao, the Legion of Doom beat
  Power & Glory, and the Beverly Brothers beat Koko B. Ware and
  Bushwhacker Luke.

MOONEY: Back to you, Vince.
