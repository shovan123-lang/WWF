PROMO - HULK HOGAN (taped insert)
Interview area. Gene Okerlund. Hogan has the WWF Title on his
shoulder and tape on his ribs.

GENE: Hulk Hogan, you walked into WrestleFest as the WWF Champion
and you walked out as the WWF Champion. But it's Sid Justice
everybody's talking about. A man you trusted.

HOGAN: Mean Gene, I'm gonna tell you something, brother. I've been
around this business long enough to know that a hand reaching out
to you can turn into a fist. Sid Justice, you pulled me up off that
mat right here on Superstars, and every Hulkamaniac in the world
thanked you for it. And then in St. Louis you buried me in front of
fifty thousand people and let that little man Wippleman put MY
belt in your hands!

You held it, Sid. Did it feel heavy, brother? 'Cause it's gonna get
a whole lot heavier. You want this title, you go to President Jack
Tunney like a man. You sign your name, and Hulk Hogan will be right
there waiting for you. And then, Sid, you're gonna find out what
it's like when twenty-four-inch pythons and all the Hulkamaniacs
run wild on YOU!

Hogan cups his ear, then walks off. Gene to camera.
