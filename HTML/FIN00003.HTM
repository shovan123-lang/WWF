NASTY BOYS PROMO
Vancouver, BC Place | Segment 3 (1:00)

Brian Knobbs and Jerry Sags stand in front of the WrestleFest backdrop, the
tag team title belts slung over their shoulders. Sags is chewing gum with
his mouth open. Knobbs leans right into the camera.

BRIAN KNOBBS: Rockers! You hear me, pretty boys? Back in Madison Square
Garden you got lucky. You got yourselves disqualified and you still walked
out of there thinking you did something.

JERRY SAGS: You didn't do nothin'! These belts never left our shoulders,
and they ain't leaving tomorrow night either!

BRIAN KNOBBS: Tomorrow in St. Louis there ain't no DQ to save you. No
referee, no hairspray, no hand holding. Just the Nasty Boys, and we're
gonna stink up Busch Stadium with your blood, Michaels!

JERRY SAGS: Nasty, baby! NASTY!

Sags shoves the camera away as both men howl.
