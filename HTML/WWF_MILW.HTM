PIPER'S BACK!
Milwaukee, Wis. | Bradley Center | July 13, 1991

The night before WrestleFest is supposed to be the quiet night. The biggest
names were resting up for St. Louis, the card was short, and the fans in
Milwaukee came out to see who'd be left standing. Then Harvey Wippleman led
Sid Justice to the ring for the main event, the house lights stayed low,
and the bagpipes started.

"Rowdy" Roddy Piper is back in a WWF ring. Piper charged down the aisle in
his kilt with the look of a man who'd been waiting a long time for this,
and he didn't wait for the bell. For sixteen minutes he and Sid fought all
over the Bradley Center, mostly on the floor, until the referee counted
both men out at 16:05. Neither man stopped when the count ended. It took a
handful of officials to pull them apart, and Sid was still glaring up the
aisle at Piper as Wippleman steered him to the back.

Why Sid? Piper didn't say before he left the building, but everyone in the
WWF has noticed Sid Justice staring at Hulk Hogan's title belt for a week.
Hogan defends that belt tomorrow night against Sgt. Slaughter. If Sid has
plans for St. Louis, he now has a Rowdy problem too.

Earlier, the Brooklyn Brawler made his WWF debut and took Virgil the full
20-minute time limit. Every time Virgil got rolling, Harvey Wippleman was
on the apron or grabbing at his boot, and the Brawler took advantage.
Virgil faces Ted DiBiase for the Million Dollar Championship tomorrow
night, and he'll have 20 hard minutes in his legs when he does.

In the six-man tag, Jimmy Snuka, Shane Douglas and Brutus Beefcake beat
Rick Martel, Dino Bravo and the Berzerker when Snuka came off the top rope
with the Superfly Splash on the Berzerker at 14:38. That's the second night
in a row Snuka has flattened the Berzerker. Dino Bravo and Rick Martel never
seemed to be on the same page in this one.

The Warlord made Bushwhacker Luke pass out in the Full Nelson at 6:57, a
warning for his WrestleFest opponent, Jimmy Snuka. Crush picked up the
biggest win of his young career, pinning Genichiro Tenryu with a
backbreaker at 8:31, the night before Tenryu and Koji Kitao meet Crush's
Demolition partners, Ax and Smash. And Koko B. Ware beat Skinner with the
Ghostbuster at 10:46, then danced all the way to the back to get ready for
the Beverly Brothers.

Tomorrow it's WrestleFest at Busch Stadium. Tonight, the whole building was
talking about the man in the kilt.
