WWF UPDATE - INJURY REPORT
WWF Studio, Stamford, CT | Segment 6 (1:30)

Sean Mooney sits behind the WWF Update desk. A graphic of the WrestleFest
'91 logo is over his shoulder.

SEAN MOONEY: Hello everyone, I'm Sean Mooney, and this is a WWF Update with
some major news heading into tomorrow night's WrestleFest.

SEAN MOONEY: This past Tuesday night in Edmonton, Alberta, two WWF
superstars suffered neck injuries. Earthquake has been ordered out of
action by WWF doctors and will not compete at WrestleFest. Stepping in to
face Jake "The Snake" Roberts will be Earthquake's partner, Typhoon.

SEAN MOONEY: Also injured that night was Greg "The Hammer" Valentine, who
was set to challenge Mr. Perfect for the Intercontinental Championship.
Valentine will be out of action for several weeks.

A photo of Owen Hart appears on screen.

SEAN MOONEY: This afternoon WWF President Jack Tunney named the new number
one contender. Owen Hart will challenge Mr. Perfect for the
Intercontinental title tomorrow night. Owen took the champion more than
eighteen minutes in Edmonton before Bobby Heenan got involved, and
President Tunney says Owen has earned his shot.

SEAN MOONEY: That means a change in the opening match as well. Bushwhacker
Luke will team with Koko B. Ware against the Beverly Brothers. All of this
tomorrow night, live from Busch Stadium.
