Match observations: 

WRESTLING CHALLENGE
Episode 1
Aired: July 21, 1991 (syndication)
Matches taped: Richfield Coliseum, Richfield, Ohio, July 18, 1991

Gorilla Monsoon and Lord Alfred Hayes opened the first Wrestling
Challenge of the summer from Richfield, and Hayes summed up the main
event before the bell: the Model, who never lets a hair fall out of
place, against a man who throws himself off the top rope for a living.

Demolition got things started, with Crush standing guard on the apron.
Smash broke Corey Sweet over his knee with a backbreaker and pinned him
at 4:03. Three men, two belts, and Hayes wasn't the only one doing the
arithmetic.

Then came the interview people will be talking about. Dino Bravo, still
sweating from his match with Tito Santana and with nobody standing
behind him, told Mean Gene Okerlund that Jimmy Hart had treated him
like a dog for years. "I am not his dog, Gene." From now on, Bravo said,
he talks for himself. The Richfield crowd cheered him, and Gorilla
admitted it was the biggest reaction Bravo has had in years.

The Big Boss Man needed 2:35 and one Boss Man Slam to put away Dong
Phillips. He didn't name anybody afterward. He didn't have to.

Power and Glory beat Willard Moody and Dick Osborne, who was back for a
second helping after the Demolition match, when Paul Roma came off the
top with a flying bodypress at 4:28. Hercules and Roma posed on the
turnbuckles like men who'd just won the tag titles. Tonight in Toronto
they get the chance to.

Brutus Beefcake opened the Barber Shop for Virgil, with the Million
Dollar Belt over his shoulder, and Rowdy Roddy Piper leaning on the
pole. Virgil said he carried Ted DiBiase's bags for years, and the belt
stays with him until DiBiase takes it back in the ring, not from behind.
Piper took care of Irwin R. Schyster's audit: he's a Scotsman, he
hasn't spent a dollar since 1974, and the only pages in his books with
I.R.S.'s name on them are IOUs for a beating. His message to DiBiase:
bring your checkbook to SummerSlam, you'll need it for the hospital
bill.

Sean Mooney's Maple Leaf Gardens Report brought the news from
Philadelphia. The Legion of Doom beat the Nasty Boys after almost an
hour on Saturday night and are the new WWF Tag Team Champions. Hawk and
Animal make their first defense tonight at the Gardens against Power
and Glory, on a card headlined by Hulk Hogan against the Warlord, with
Sid Justice in the Warlord's corner.

The main event was the best thing on the show. Jimmy Snuka took it to
Rick Martel early with chops and a headbutt, and Martel bailed to the
floor to fix his hair. Martel took over with a knee on the way back in
and went to work on the back, until the Superfly fought back with chops
in the corner and a diving headbutt that nearly ended it. Snuka slammed
Martel and went up top, but Martel rolled clear, and with the referee
checking on Snuka, Martel went to the eye. The Boston crab went on in
the middle of the ring, and after two reaches for the ropes, Snuka
submitted at 9:47.

Martel checked his face in a hand mirror before he would talk to Gene.
Not a mark on it. Class beats a crowd, he said, and then he had a word
for SATO: excitement fades, but arrogance is forever. He sprayed the
cologne into the air and walked off through the mist.
