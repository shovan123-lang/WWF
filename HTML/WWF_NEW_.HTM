Match observations: 

New York, New York Madison Square Garden July 1st,1991 |
Average Ticket Price: $35.00 Attendance:15,520 | Gate $543,200

MADISON SQUARE GARDEN ROCKS AS WARRIOR SURVIVES THE DARKNESS!

New York City was electric on July 1 as 15,520 roaring fans packed the
world famous Madison Square Garden for a night of mayhem, muscle, and
madness that only the World Wrestling Federation can deliver. With
tensions boiling over across the roster, the Garden faithful witnessed
shocking upsets, wild brawls, and a main event that pushed two titans to
their absolute limits.

The evening kicked off with a flurry of interviews, as Earthquake vowed
to crush "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan, while The Undertaker and Paul Bearer
ominously promised to seal The Ultimate Warrior inside a custom built
casket. The mood was tense, the stakes high.

In the ring,

Dino Bravo muscled past a fiery Shane Douglas, while Ricky "The Dragon"
Steamboat delivered a show stealing performance, soaring through the air
to defeat the mighty Hercules in a no countout thriller that had the
crowd on its feet.

The monstrous Berzerker continued his path of destruction by dispatching
Superfly Jimmy Snuka, and the tag team division erupted when The Nasty
Boys retained their WWF Tag Team Championship after chaos broke loose
between The Rockers, Jimmy Hart, and the officials.

The powerhouses kept swinging as The Warlord forced Greg "The Hammer
Valentine to submit to his crushing Full Nelson, and Hacksaw Jim Duggan
shocked the Garden by flooring Earthquake with the Three Point Stance —
despite the announced no show of Jake "The Snake" Roberts.

Haku & The Barbarian battled The Orient Express to a disqualification
filled war of interference, with both Bobby Heenan and Mr. Fuji stirring
trouble at ringside.

But the night belonged to the Casket Match.

The Ultimate Warrior and The Undertaker waged a supernatural war of
endurance. Warrior fought through Paul Bearer's interference, the
darkness of the casket, and the relentless assault of the Deadman.

In the end, Warrior summoned every ounce of power to slam the casket shut
on The Undertaker, scoring a victory that shook MSG to its core.

The Garden crowd left buzzing — some stunned, some thrilled, all knowing
they had witnessed a night of WWF action they wouldn't soon forget.

WWF Live Event: Madison Square Garden

Merchandise: Strong Warrior, Savage, and Undertaker sales

Crowd Energy: Hot early, peaked during Steamboat/Hercules and the Casket
Match

Dino Bravo vs. Shane Douglas

Solid opener; Bravo got heat, Douglas showed good fire. Short match but
effective for pacing.

Douglas continues to improve; consider more undercard reps.

Ricky Steamboat vs. Hercules — No Countout**

Best wrestling match of the night.

Steamboat extremely over; crowd invested in every comeback.

Hercules looked strong; finish protected both.

Recommend Steamboat for future MSG dates.

Berzerker vs. Jimmy Snuka

Berzerker's push continues; Snuka still draws nostalgia reactions.

Match was fine; Berzerker's gimmick connects with kids.

Nasty Boys vs. Rockers — WWF Tag Team Championship

Good action; DQ finish kept the belts on the Nasties while protecting
Rockers. Jimmy Hart interference got strong heat. Rockers remain one of
the most popular acts on the card.

Warlord vs. Greg Valentine surprisingly solid; Valentine got the crowd
behind him.

Warlord's Full Nelson finish looked strong.

Mid card power match that delivered.

Jim Duggan vs. Earthquake

Duggan remains a reliable babyface for MSG.

Earthquake took the loss but stayed strong due to match length.

Jake Roberts no show noted; may need storyline adjustment.

Haku & Barbarian vs. Orient Express

Heavy interference from both managers; crowd reacted well.

DQ finish keeps feud alive. Barbarian especially over with the live
crowd.

Casket Match — Ultimate Warrior vs. The Undertaker

Extremely long match; crowd stayed surprisingly engaged.

Warrior's win got a huge pop; Undertaker protected by Paul Bearer's
involvement.

Recommend shorter pacing for future casket matches on house shows.

General Notes

Strong house show business; MSG remains a reliable draw.

Warrior, Savage (via Barbershop segment), and Undertaker were top merch
movers.

Consider Warrior vs. Undertaker rematch with tighter timing.

Steamboat should be featured more prominently; consistently strong
reactions.
