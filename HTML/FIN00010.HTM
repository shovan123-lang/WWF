PAUL BEARER'S FUNERAL PARLOR
Vancouver, BC Place | Segment 12 (1:30)

The lights dim. Organ music plays as the camera pans across the Funeral
Parlor set: candles, lilies, and an open casket. Paul Bearer stands beside
it holding the urn. The Undertaker stands motionless in the shadows behind
him.

PAUL BEARER: Ohhh yesss. Welcome, my friends, to the Funeral Parlor.
Ultimate Warrior, you won three times. In New York, in Des Moines, in
Anaheim. Three times you thought you buried the Undertaker.

PAUL BEARER: But the Undertaker does not stay buried, Warrior. The
Undertaker always comes back. And tomorrow night in St. Louis, the Warrior
finally rests in peace.

Bearer runs his hand along the edge of the casket. The Undertaker steps
forward into the light.

THE UNDERTAKER: Warrior. Tomorrow night, your fear becomes my power. Rest
in peace.

Bearer raises the urn and lets out a long, shrill laugh as the lights go
out.
