HARVEY WIPPLEMAN & SID JUSTICE
Toronto, Maple Leaf Gardens | Segment 7, backstage (1:00)

Sean Mooney. Harvey Wippleman, in his tuxedo, stands in front of Sid
Justice, who fills the whole frame behind him and doesn't look at the
camera.

SEAN MOONEY: Harvey Wippleman, your man the Warlord faces Hulk Hogan in
tonight's main event, and you've said Sid Justice will be in his
corner.

HARVEY WIPPLEMAN: That's right, Mooney. The Warlord gets his shot at
the WWF Champion tonight, and Sid Justice gets the best seat in the
house. He's going to stand there, and he's going to watch. He's going
to learn every move Hulk Hogan makes. And when Jack Tunney finally
gives us what we've been asking for, Sid will already know how this
ends.

SEAN MOONEY: Sid, do you have anything to say to Hulk Hogan?

Sid looks at the camera for the first time.

SID JUSTICE: Look up, Hogan. I'll be there.

Sid walks off. Wippleman hurries after him.
