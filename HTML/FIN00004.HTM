Sean Mooney. Dino Bravo walks in alone, no robe, no manager. The crowd
noise from the arena carries into the hallway. He listens to it for a

for the first time since Jimmy Hart fired you.

DINO BRAVO: Mooney, for a long time I came to Canada and nobody knew
which Dino Bravo they would get. Tonight they know. I come to the ring
alone. I win alone.

Skinner wants to fight me in his alligator boots. Good. I grew up in
Montreal. I have seen worse things come out of the swamp in the spring.

(in French, to the camera) Ce soir, c'est pour vous.

He walks off toward the ring.

SEAN MOONEY: "Tonight is for you." Dino Bravo, on his own.
