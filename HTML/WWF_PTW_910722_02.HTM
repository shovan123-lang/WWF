MATCH 1 - PRIME TIME EXCLUSIVE (taped Richfield 7/18)

Tito Santana vs. Dino Bravo (w/ Jimmy Hart)

Finish: flying forearm. Tito pins at 10:45. **

(14:00 with entrances and the angle)

Layout

  • Hart barks instructions through the megaphone from the floor. Bravo never looks at him.
  • Bravo uses the power early: shoulder blocks, a side headlock, a long bearhug in the middle. Tito punches his way out.
  • Tito comeback: armdrags, the punches in the corner, an atomic drop for two.
  • Bravo cuts him off with the side suplex for two. Hart pounds the apron.
  • Hart climbs onto the apron and holds the megaphone out to Bravo. Bravo shakes his head and turns around into the flying forearm. One, two, three.

POST-MATCH ANGLE (taped Richfield 7/18)

  • Hart storms into the ring screaming, megaphone in Bravo's face, and jabs a finger at the back: you're through. Fired.
  • Bravo stares at him while Hart keeps yelling.
  • Bravo lifts Hart over his head and press slams him over the top rope to the floor. Big pop.
  • Bravo walks to the back alone. Tito watches from the aisle.

Studio over the footage

  • MOONEY: Tito beat the Mountie at WrestleFest, Bobby, and he's carried it right into Richfield.
  • HEENAN: Look at Jimmy on the apron. All these years in this business and he still thinks that megaphone is a weapon.
  • On the finish, HEENAN: "He turned around to look at Jimmy Hart. Never look at your manager, Mooney. Look at me, fine. Not at Jimmy."
  • On the press slam, MOONEY: "Dino Bravo just threw Jimmy Hart out of the ring!" HEENAN: "Best thing anybody's done with Jimmy Hart in years."
