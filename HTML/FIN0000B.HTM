SUPERSTARS OF WRESTLING - EPISODE 2 - COLD OPEN
WrestleFest '91 highlight package (2:00)

Footage: Busch Stadium, St. Louis, July 14. More than 51,000 fans.
Hulk Hogan drops the big leg on Sgt. Slaughter. Special referee
Hacksaw Jim Duggan counts three. Hogan holds the WWF Title high.

Cut to: Sid Justice and Harvey Wippleman come through the crowd.
Sid levels Duggan from behind, then turns on Hogan and lays him out.
Wippleman picks up the WWF Title and hands it to Sid. Sid holds it
over the fallen champion.

Cut to: the Legion of Doom hit the ring. Sid drops the belt and
backs up the aisle, smiling the whole way.

VINCE (V/O): One week ago, Hulk Hogan survived Sgt. Slaughter in
front of more than fifty thousand fans. And then Sid Justice, the
man who saved Hulk Hogan right here on Superstars, showed us who he
really is. Welcome to Superstars, everybody. I'm Vince McMahon,
alongside the "Macho Man" Randy Savage. Macho, what a week.

SAVAGE: Oooh yeah! A week where you find out who your friends are,
Vince! One week Sid Justice is standing next to the Hulkster. The
next week he's standing over him! The cream rises to the top, and
