Match observations: 

ALL AMERICAN WRESTLING
Aired: July 21, 1991 (USA Network)
Matches taped: Richfield Coliseum, Richfield, Ohio, July 18, 1991

Gene Okerlund and Hillbilly Jim hosted an hour built around three
matches from Richfield that aired nowhere else.

Brutus Beefcake put Jerry Simmons to sleep in 3:06 and then went to
work with the shears, which took longer than the match.

Bobby Heenan stopped by the studio to repeat his SummerSlam promise:
Mr. Perfect will defend the Intercontinental Title against anybody.
Asked about Haku and the Barbarian facing the Legion of Doom on Prime
Time, the Brain waved it off. "They know where the ring is."

Virgil made Dong Phillips submit to a cobra clutch in 2:06, a new
hold Ted DiBiase will want to study. I.R.S. ran in afterward and
made off with the Million Dollar Belt, but Rowdy Roddy Piper met him
on the ramp, knocked him down and handed the belt back to Virgil.

Sean Mooney's injury report had the Ultimate Warrior, Ricky
Steamboat, Earthquake and Greg Valentine on the shelf, with
Bushwhacker Butch cleared to return. The show re-aired the
WrestleFest match of the night, Tito Santana's win over the Mountie,
and Hacksaw Jim Duggan promised Sgt. Slaughter a 2x4 in Toronto.

In the main event, SATO followed Paul Roma to the top rope and
brought him down with a superplex at 7:03. Afterward SATO told Mr.
Perfect he'd be watching Bobby Heenan in Toronto tonight, and taking
the belt home.
