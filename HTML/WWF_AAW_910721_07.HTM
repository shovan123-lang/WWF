WRESTLEFEST REWIND (re-air, 7:00)
WrestleFest '91, Busch Stadium, St. Louis, July 14.
Tito Santana vs. The Mountie (w/ Jimmy Hart). Edited to fit.
Gene and Jim voice it over from the studio.
As it happened: Tito pins the Mountie with the flying forearm.
***3/4, match of the night.

GENE (intro): One week ago in St. Louis, more than 51,000 fans saw a
lot of great matches. Most people agree this was the best of them.

Layout of the edit:
- The Mountie and Jimmy Hart stall at ringside while Tito fires up
  the crowd.
- Tito controls early with armdrags and the punches in the corner.
- Hart trips Tito from the floor and the Mountie takes over, working
  the neck.
- The Mountie locks in the Carotid Control. Tito's arm drops twice,
  and he fights out.
- Tito ducks a clothesline, hits the ropes, flying forearm.
  One, two, three.
- Postscript footage from St. Louis: the Mountie's shock stick on the
  Big Boss Man.

Studio over the footage:
- GENE: The Mountie beat Tito in Anaheim with the Carotid Control.
  Tito hadn't forgotten.
- JIM: And look at Jimmy Hart, Gene, trying to get his fingers in it.
- After the pin, JIM: "That's El Matador! Tito's been on a roll,
  Gene."
- On the shock stick, GENE: "That's the Mountie for you. And the Big
  Boss Man has a long memory." Leave it there.
