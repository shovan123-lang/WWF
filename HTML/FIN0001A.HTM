OWEN HART PROMO
Winnipeg, Winnipeg Arena (recorded 7/12) | Segment 7 (1:00)

Mean Gene stands with Owen Hart, who is still sweating from a match, a
towel around his neck.

MEAN GENE: Owen Hart, a few hours ago President Jack Tunney made it
official. Tomorrow night you challenge Mr. Perfect for the Intercontinental
Championship. What's going through your mind?

OWEN HART: Gene, I wish it was under different circumstances, and I hope
Greg Valentine gets well soon. But I'm not going to pretend I don't want
this.

OWEN HART: Edmonton, Alberta. Eighteen minutes in the ring with Mr.
Perfect, and I had him beat. I had him beat until that weasel Bobby Heenan
grabbed my boot.

OWEN HART: So Heenan, tomorrow night you can bring a ladder and a step
stool for all I care. Perfect, I'm not a stepping stone and I'm not
anybody's little brother. When I leave St. Louis, I'm leaving with that
Intercontinental belt around my waist.

Owen pulls the towel off his neck and walks out of frame.
