BIG BOSS MAN WITH MEAN GENE
Vancouver, BC Place | Segment 16 (1:00)

Mean Gene stands with Big Boss Man, nightstick in hand and handcuffs on his
belt.

lawman does.

BIG BOSS MAN: And Mountie, you and me, we got business coming. Count on it.

Boss Man slaps the nightstick into his palm and walks off.
