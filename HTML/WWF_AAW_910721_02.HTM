MATCH 1 - AAW EXCLUSIVE (taped Richfield 7/18)
Brutus "The Barber" Beefcake vs. Jerry Simmons
Finish: sleeper. Simmons submits at 3:06. Then the haircut.
(6:30 with the entrance and the haircut)

Layout:
- Beefcake comes out snipping the shears at the front row.
- Simmons jumps him at the bell and gets a few forearms in.
  Beefcake shrugs them off: strut, atomic drop, clothesline.
- Sleeper in the middle. Simmons' arm drops once, twice, and he
  gives up before the third.
- Beefcake drags Simmons to the corner and goes to work with the
  shears while the crowd counts the snips. Hair all over the mat.

Studio over the footage:
- GENE: Brutus has had a rough month, Jim. Crush in Anaheim, Sid
  Justice in Edmonton.
- JIM: Well, he sure took it out on Jerry Simmons' hairdo.
- GENE: And let's not forget the Barber Shop at WrestleFest, with
  Ricky Steamboat, until Skinner barged in.
- On the haircut, JIM: "Took him longer to cut that hair than to
  win the match, Gene."
