THE BARBER SHOP (taped Richfield 7/18, 4:00)
The Barber Shop set: barber chair, striped pole, plate glass window
with "BARBER SHOP" painted on it. Brutus Beefcake with his shears.
Virgil sits in the chair with the Million Dollar Belt over his
shoulder. Piper leans on the pole.

BEEFCAKE: Welcome to the Barber Shop! I've got a man in my chair today
who used to carry another man's bags. Now he's carrying the man's
belt. Virgil, how does it feel?

VIRGIL: Brutus, for years I stood behind Ted DiBiase. I held his coat,
I carried his money, I smiled when he laughed at me. In St. Louis he
beat me, and he wanted me to hand this belt back to him and say thank
you. I didn't. This belt stays with me until he takes it from me in
that ring. Not from behind. Not with his flunkies. In that ring.

BEEFCAKE: And you've got a friend.

PIPER: A friend? I'm his insurance policy, Brutus! And I hear Irwin R.
Schyster wants to audit me. Irwin! Ha! I'm a Scotsman! I haven't spent
a dollar since 1974! You want to go through my books, laddie? You'll
find a lot of pages with your name on them, and every one of them is
an IOU for an ass-kickin'!

DiBiase, you said everybody has a price. Here's mine: nothin'. You
can't buy me, you can't buy him, and you're not gettin' that belt
back. SummerSlam, Ted. Bring your checkbook. You're gonna need it for
the hospital bill.

Beefcake snips the air with the shears at the camera. Piper slaps
Virgil on the back. Virgil holds the belt up.

Booth:
HAYES: Mr. Piper does have a way with words.
GORILLA: For the first time, Virgil is standing on his own two feet.
